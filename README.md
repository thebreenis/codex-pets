A collection of custom animated pets.

## Pets

| Pet | Download | Files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meat Boy | [Download ZIP](downloads/meat-boy.zip) | [Source files](pets/meat-boy/) |

## Adding pets

Store each pet in `pets/<pet-name>/` with `pet.json` and `spritesheet.webp`. Add its ZIP to `downloads/` and link it in the table above.

Each ZIP must be under 5 MB and contain `pet.json` and `spritesheet.webp` directly at its root.

Meat Boy is unofficial fan art. Character rights belong to their respective owners.
